About Me

Designer. Storyteller. Problem solver.

I'm Siddharth — a graphic designer with a sharp eye for concept-led work. From brand campaigns for Apple and Garmin concepts to event posters and motion graphics teasers, I build visuals that carry an idea all the way through.

Selected Work

Projects

01
Brand Campaign · Concept

Apple Create — "Same Idea. New Tools." (Poster 1)

A concept campaign for Apple's creative tools division. This poster uses the evolution from classical sheet music notation to a digital audio waveform — showing that creativity hasn't changed, only the tools have. Clean, minimal, and unmistakably Apple.

02
Brand Campaign · Concept

Apple Create — "Same Idea. New Tools." (Poster 2)

The companion piece to the first. A split visual contrasting a hand-drawn sketchbook page with a fully rendered digital world on a smartphone screen — the same character, two eras. The collage texture and mixed media feel give it a warmth that balances the digital side perfectly.

03
Ad Campaign · Concept

Garmin — Travel Brilliantly

Part of a three-poster concept campaign for Garmin's Smart Performance Series. A glowing neon route traced across a dark mountain aerial — dramatic, data-rich, and visceral. Designed to feel like the watch itself is narrating the adventure.

04
Ad Campaign · Concept

Garmin — Train Brilliantly

A single luminous trail cutting through a cracked-earth texture. The route glows red-to-orange at peak intensity, mirroring the effort data shown alongside it. Sparse stats, maximum impact.

05
Ad Campaign · Concept

Garmin — Beyond Limits

The third piece in the Garmin series. Pure performance data turned into art — 200+ BPM, VO₂ max, pace, calories — laid out with the confidence of a billboard. "Measure What Matters" as both tagline and design principle.

06
Motion Graphics · Teaser

VIRUS — Movie Teaser

A fully original concept built from imagination — no brief, no client, just the idea: what if a virus became a film? VIRUS is a motion graphics teaser created entirely in After Effects, with glitch typography, terminal UI elements, and a creeping sense of system failure. The poster is just a frame from something much bigger. Want to see the full teaser? Get in touch.

07
Event Branding · Poster

Porto Coffee Festival 2026

A vintage typographic poster for PCF 2026. Warm chocolate tones, retro letterpress layering, and mixed type scales create a tactile warmth that puts you in the room before you've even smelled the coffee. The balance of serif, script, and display faces is deliberate — each word has its own personality.

08
Event Branding · Poster

Berline Electronics Dance Music Festival

An explosive paint-splash composition in deep magenta for a Berlin electronic music festival. The contrast between the heavy uppercase "DANCE MUSIC" and the fluid brush-script "Festival" creates the same tension the event promises on the floor. Energy you can feel through the screen.

09
Event Branding · Poster

Barcelona Architecture Exhibition — January 2026

A structured geometric composition using bold primary colours referencing Catalonian architectural heritage. The deliberate use of blue, crimson and gold in simple volumetric blocks mirrors the exhibition's subject — architecture as the art of assembling forms. Clean, confident, and built to last.
